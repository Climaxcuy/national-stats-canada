Canada in Numbers

One country, 41 million people, the world's second-largest landmass — measured with real, sourced figures on population, the economy, the provinces, resources and climate.

Figures: 2024 · Sources cited on every number

Population growth, 2015–2024

Canada passed 40 million people in 2023 and 41 million in 2024 — the fastest growth in the G7, driven mostly by immigration.

The economy at a glance

A US$2.24 trillion economy in 2024 — 9th in the world — powered by energy, vehicles, minerals and food.

Three things the numbers say

Four in ten Canadians live in OntarioOntario holds 16.1 million of 41.3 million people (39%). Quebec is next at 9.1 million (22%).
Energy pays the billsCrude oil alone was C$158.2 billion of exports in 2024 — 21.9% of everything Canada sold abroad.
Two-thirds of electricity is waterHydropower supplies about 66% of Canada's electricity; renewables together supply 69%.
