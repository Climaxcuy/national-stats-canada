      About this site

      What it is

      Canada in Numbers is an independent educational portal that gathers Canada's headline national statistics — population, economy, provinces, resources, climate and trade — into one readable place, with the source and year attached to every number.

      It is built as a fully static site: HTML, CSS and JavaScript files only, no server, no tracking, no network requests, no external fonts. All charts are drawn as inline SVG from the data file.

      What it is not

      It is not an official statistics site and is not affiliated with Statistics Canada, the Government of Canada, the World Bank, the IEA or Environment and Climate Change Canada. For authoritative data, always consult the source institution directly — the links in the register above take you there.

      Figures are shown as published. Where a value is an estimate (for example, July 1 population estimates), the page says so.
