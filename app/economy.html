Economy

A US$2.24 trillion economy in 2024 — 9th in the world. Real growth was 1.6% in 2024, inflation averaged 2.4%, and unemployment 6.3%.

What Canada sells

Merchandise exports totalled C$721.1 billion in 2024; imports were C$765.7 billion. Energy products alone were C$176.2 billion of exports, and crude oil — C$158.2 billion — was the single largest export, 21.9% of the total.

Who Canada trades with

The United States took 75.9% of Canada's exports in 2024, and two-way goods trade with the US passed C$1 trillion for a third straight year. The trade surplus with the US was C$102.3 billion.

Why energy dominates: Canada is the world's fourth-largest oil producer and a top exporter of crude oil, natural gas, potash, gold and lumber.
