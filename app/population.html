Population

41,288,599 people on July 1, 2024 — up 3.0% in a single year. Four in ten live in Ontario; the three territories together hold fewer people than a small city.

Statistics Canada · July 1, 2024

A decade of growth

From 35.7 million in 2015 to 41.3 million in 2024 — growth of 5.6 million people in nine years, the fastest sustained growth since the 1950s.

What drove it? In 2023, Canada's population grew 3.2% — a rate unprecedented among developed nations — almost entirely through immigration and non-permanent residents. Natural increase (births minus deaths) contributed only a small share. Source: Statistics Canada, The Daily.
